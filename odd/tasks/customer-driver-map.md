## Specs

S1. While the driver's route screen is open and the route has at least one active stop, the driver app reports its position to `PUT /api/v1/drivers/me/location` every 15 seconds. Foreground only: no background tracking (ADR 0007).
S2. The app asks for location permission when the route screen opens. If the driver refuses, the screen says tracking is off and the rest of the screen keeps working.
S3. The customer snapshot `GET /api/v1/orders/{id}/tracking` includes `driverPosition: {latitude, longitude, recordedAt}` only when the driver's last position is fresh. Otherwise `driverPosition` is null. Assumption A1: fresh means recorded within the last 2 minutes.
S4. The snapshot never includes the driver's identity: no id, name, or phone.
S5. The customer's order screen shows a map with the delivery point and the driver's position, plus the distance in km and the ETA in minutes when they are available.
S6. Out of scope: optimal route ordering, route geometry, and the stop result endpoint (`POST .../result`, still 501).

User approval (verbatim, Spanish): "lo apruebo", answering the question about showing the driver's position on the customer's map with distance and ETA.

Decision D5 (L10, user), closes the previous open question:
- Wrong address reported by the driver at the delivery point: the surcharge is charged (no operator step before charging, the driver's report with GPS within the radius is the evidence).
- Wrong address notified by the customer before dispatch: no surcharge; the customer can reschedule the date.
- Repeat offender: the first customer-caused non-delivery (any reason caused by the customer) opens a 2-week window; a third one inside that window marks the customer as a repeat offender. The operator may then apply the charge with a written justification; it stays pending audit. Purpose: identify customers who abuse the app.
- Appeal: the customer may contest a charged non-delivery through the existing dispute (ADR 0007 section 6, 2-hour window) and agree with the operator a reassignment to another day with a reduced charge. A resolved appeal still counts toward the threshold.
- Driver already en route when the customer gives a reason: the delivery is reassigned, with an extra travel charge paid by the customer. The travel charge is calculated by distance, using the current gasoline price as reference, and an administrator can adjust it. The same travel charge is included in a wrong-address charge. D3 (reassignment with no charge) applies only before dispatch.
- Evidence: no photo. When the driver registers NOT_DELIVERED, the driver's position is sent with the order information; the same happens on DELIVERED, to confirm the delivery and feed the operator's audit metrics. The customer's in-app confirmation is the second source.
